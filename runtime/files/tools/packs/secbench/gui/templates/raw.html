Raw — extra helper args, power-user escape hatch

Text saved here is split on whitespace and appended verbatim to the selected module's command line every time it is started (after its form fields and --count/--interval). Useful for a helper flag that has no dedicated form field yet. The --iface flag is always fixed to eth1 and cannot be overridden here — extra args are appended, not prepended, and every helper re-checks its own iface argument regardless (see attacks/common.py).
